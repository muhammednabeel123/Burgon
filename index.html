Our Story

CRAFTED WITH FIRE,
SERVED WITH PRIDE

Born in the heart of Thiruvananthapuram, Burgon started with one obsession — the perfect burger. We grind our patties in-house every morning, bake our brioche fresh, and layer in flavours inspired by Kerala's spice heritage.

Every burger is flame-grilled to order over an open fire. No shortcuts, no freezers, no compromise. That's the revolution.
